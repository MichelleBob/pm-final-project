# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** Riverty Case Study: BU Collection — A Fair Way Forward
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Bad Consumer Feedback may lead to the loss of clients and weakens our market position. Inside of our company, there will be more failed instalment plans and less payments, which means our income is lower.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is forced to contact us more often and gives bad user feedback on other platforms. Her made instalment plan is failing, why she needs to call us again.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Today's journey loses money and agent capacity at two points: before an agreement is reached, and after it. Left unchanged, costs rise with volume, portfolio value erodes, and the headline metric (SSR) can mask the problem.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Create a transparent, guided repayment journey that helps consumers choose sustainable instalment plans the first time, reducing unnecessary agent interactions while improving long-term recovery performance beyond SSR.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** It's a high-stakes threat because it has direct impact on the success of instalment plans and repayability of debts. With this clients could jump off, because they don't get their money back from the users. Also our costs will rise because of higher amount of contacts and calls.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** It is a systemic problem because it has direct impact on our amount of clients and on our costs and our income.

Assumptions on the problem: 
- Average open balance per case: €600
- Share of plan value ultimately collected on a kept plan: 70%
- Value lost on a broken plan: 30% of balance
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** It removes the obstacles because it makes it easier for the user to get an instalment plan and to be on track with it. This has an impact on the amount of user contacts and on the happiness of our clients.
