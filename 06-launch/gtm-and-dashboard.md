# GTM Launch Plan, Riverty business-unit case

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Free amount input alongside the slider: Typing a number lets a variable-income debtor enter their own figure instead of dragging from the 150 anchor. |
| Goal | Conversion |
| Launch tier | L, Multi-channel |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Conversion, We want to increase the amount of successful payments inside the instalment process.
- **Target audience:** Debtors with variable income and multiple claims who are looking for a quick answer.

## Launch Tier
- **L, Multi-channel**, With the feature we reach all users that are not able to pay their claim off at ones, The impact is high, because a failed instalment plan means in most cases, that no other one can be made afterwards. Silence would risk that more claims and instalment plans are failing.

## Channels
1. **Owned: Consumer Service Portal inside the instalment plan**
2. **Owned: Emails with reminders to pay and change their instalment rate**
3. **Owned: Telephone when someone is calling**

## Enablement & Assets
Call agent training, letter changes

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM  of the portal, PM of the communication teams to add the emails - email changes and sending them out cost extra, Data analysts to evaluate the data
- **Timeline:** Phase 1 change the portal and train the agents, Phase 2 change the letters, Phase 3 evaluate the data and make decisions

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** - Increased number of successful instalment plan creation
- Increased number of successful payments of instalment rates
- Increased number of paid off claims
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Increased number of failed instalment plan creation and paid instalment rates
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Change the form or leave it like it was planned
